<i>Usugami</i> (薄紙, Japanese for "thin paper") turns photos, camera scans, Word documents, PowerPoint slides, Excel spreadsheets and EPUB e-books into PDFs, and has the tools you need for the PDFs you already have. Everything happens on your phone: Usugami has no internet permission, so nothing is ever uploaded.

<b>Create PDFs</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Scan document:</b> take photos with your phone's camera app, or upload images. Crop each page straight (Usugami can find it, even a white page on a white desk) and turn it, then give it a look: original, lighten, enhanced, super enhanced, grayscale, black &amp; white (about 10 KB a page) or ink saver, with its brightness and contrast. Apply to all pages gives every page the same look, brightness and contrast.</li>
<li><b>Images to PDF:</b> JPG and PNG images from your gallery or files, cropped and cleaned up the same way if you like. Choose the page size, orientation and margin, and make one PDF or one per image. Images are always compressed, and their location and other hidden metadata are removed.</li>
<li><b>Text recognition (OCR)</b> makes the PDF searchable, in English, French, Spanish and Hindi. It is open source and built in (PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv5 models, run by Tencent's ncnn), so it works offline, always.</li>
<li><b>Word, PowerPoint, Excel and EPUB to PDF:</b> .docx, .pptx, .xlsx and .epub files converted on your phone, with their styles, tables and pictures, headers and footers, and the book's table of contents as bookmarks. Spreadsheets come out sheet by sheet as Excel prints them, with their number and date formats.</li>
</ul>

<b>PDF tools</b>
<ul>
<li>Split, merge, reorganize and rotate PDFs, and delete pages</li>
<li>Compress PDFs (it never makes a PDF bigger)</li>
<li>Edit PDFs: add text, drawings, highlights, shapes and pictures</li>
<li>Sign with your handwritten signature</li>
<li>Add page numbers or a watermark</li>
<li>Flatten forms and comments</li>
<li>Redact for real: what is under the black boxes is removed from the file, not just covered</li>
<li>Open password-protected PDFs</li>
</ul>

<b>Batch mode:</b> compress, number, watermark or flatten many PDFs at once, or convert many Word, PowerPoint, Excel or EPUB files, each into a PDF of its own. Save or share the new PDFs one by one or as one ZIP file.

<b>Share to Usugami:</b> share images, PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel or EPUB files from any app, and they open in the right tool.

<b>Recent files:</b> the files you save are listed on Home, to open or share again.

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission, and no permissions to grant</li>
<li>No ads, no tracking, no account</li>
<li>No Google code: Google Play services are not needed</li>
<li>Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later)</li>
</ul>

In English, French, Spanish and Hindi, with light and dark themes. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Don't need text recognition? <i>Usugami Lite</i> has every other tool and is even smaller.
