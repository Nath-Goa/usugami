<i>Usugami Lite</i> turns photos, camera scans, ID cards, Word documents, PowerPoint slides, Excel spreadsheets and EPUB e-books into PDFs, opens PDFs and documents in its own reader, and has the tools you need for the PDFs you already have. It has every tool of <i>Usugami</i> (薄紙, Japanese for "thin paper") except text recognition, so it is even smaller. Everything happens on your phone: Usugami Lite has no internet permission, so nothing is ever uploaded.

<b>Create PDFs</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Scan document:</b> take photos with your phone's camera app, or upload images. Crop each page straight (Usugami Lite can find it, even a white page on a white desk), then give it a look: original, lighten, enhanced, super enhanced, grayscale, black &amp; white or ink saver.</li>
<li><b>ID card:</b> the front and the back of a card on one page, at the card's real size.</li>
<li><b>Images to PDF:</b> JPG, PNG, HEIC, AVIF, WebP, BMP and GIF images, cropped and cleaned up the same way if you like, in one PDF or one per image. Images are always compressed, and their location and other hidden metadata are removed.</li>
<li><b>Documents to PDF:</b> Word, PowerPoint, Excel and EPUB files converted on your phone, with their styles, tables and pictures.</li>
</ul>

<b>Read and annotate</b>
<ul>
<li>Open PDFs and Word, Excel, PowerPoint and EPUB files from any app with "Open with"</li>
<li>Zoom, find text, select and copy text, share and print</li>
<li>Annotate with text, drawings, highlights, shapes and pictures, saved as a new PDF</li>
</ul>

<b>PDF tools</b>
<ul>
<li>Split, merge and reorganize PDFs: move, turn, delete and add pages</li>
<li>Page layout: crop pages, or put 2, 4, 6 or 9 pages on a sheet</li>
<li>Compress PDFs, or turn them black and white</li>
<li>Sign, and add page numbers or a watermark</li>
<li>Flatten forms and comments</li>
<li>Redact for real: what is under the black boxes is removed, not just covered</li>
<li>Document info: see, change or remove the title, author and more</li>
<li>PDF to images or text: pages as JPG or PNG, or their text</li>
<li>Print, and open password-protected PDFs</li>
</ul>

<b>Batch mode:</b> compress, number, watermark, flatten or convert many files at once, and save or share the new PDFs as one ZIP file.

<b>Share to Usugami Lite</b> from any app, and files open in the right tool. <b>Recent files</b> keeps what you saved at hand, and a long-press on the app's icon opens four tools straight away.

<b>Usugami watermark:</b> to support the app, a switch adds a small "Usugami" to your new PDFs. It is off until you turn it on.

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission, and no permissions to grant</li>
<li>No ads, no tracking, no account</li>
<li>No Google code: Google Play services are not needed</li>
<li>Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later)</li>
</ul>

In English, French, Spanish, Hindi, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Indonesian, with light and dark themes. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Want searchable scans? <i>Usugami</i> has the same tools plus open-source text recognition (OCR) in seven languages. Usugami Lite is a separate app, so the two can be installed side by side.
